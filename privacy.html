Privacy Policy

HA Nest Link is a personal, single-user integration connecting one individual's Home Assistant instance to their own Google Nest devices. It is not distributed publicly and has no other users.

Data accessed (Nest device status and controls) is used solely to display and control the owner's own devices within their own private Home Assistant instance. No data is shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party.
